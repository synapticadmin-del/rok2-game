1. Crusader camps are open for contending every 12 hours. During this period, all alliances may attack the crusader camp. Any alliance who can occupy and hold a crusader camp for 4 hours will gain control of it.
2. The first commander (captain of the rallied army) to occupy a crusader camp will become the defense captain responsible for repelling enemy attacks. If this commander is withdrawn, the commander with the highest power will automatically succeed the position.
3. Buffs from the same types of crusader camps do not stack.
4. Half of the units who are severely wounded during crusader camp battles will be sent to the hospital, while the rest will die immediately (during battles with barbarians all severely wounded units will be sent to the hospital).
