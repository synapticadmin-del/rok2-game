1. Forming an Army Group
 - If your alliance is in the Season of Conquest, you can create alliance army groups.
 - You can only control 1 army group at a time. An army group can hold up to 7 troops.
 - Your alliance can have up to 10 army groups at a time.
2. Controlling Army Groups
 - Control of all troops in an army group will be turned over to the creator of the army group.
 - Army groups can only be used to attack other governors' troops.
 - Army groups cannot enter certain special areas, such as Altars of Darkness.
 - Army groups cannot use certain story-related features, such as unit skills in the Keener Blades story.
 - When an army group travels over long distances, it will travel at the March Speed of the slowest troop in the army group.
3. Disbanding Army Groups
 - If a troop in an army group is defeated, it will automatically withdraw from the army group.
 - Army groups will automatically disband if they have no troops remaining.
 - Your alliance leader and members with the required permissions can disband the army groups of other alliance members.
 - If the servers go offline, all army groups will automatically disband.

<b><size=18>Obtaining Alliance Armory Resources</size></b>
<b>1. How to obtain resources</b>
- When alliance members <b><color=#FF7800>defeat Barbarians/Barbarian Forts, give alliance help, donate alliance technologies, gather resources, or shop at the Courier Station</color></b>, there is a chance of discovering armory resources. These found resources will automatically be added to the alliance armory. The daily quota for resources that each alliance member can contribute is <b><color=#FF7800>{p1}</color></b>.
- Certain alliance events may also give armory resources directly. These resources are automatically added to the alliance armory.
<b>2. Resource acquisition limitations</b>
- Only the following resource types can be added to the alliance armory via the aforementioned methods: Food, Wood, Stone, and Gold.
- <b><color=#FF7800>Flux Coins are considered a special resource</color></b>, and they can only be donated by alliance members who have reached VIP level {p3}.
<b><size=18>Alliance Bounty Rules</size></b>
<b>1. Posting a bounty</b>
The alliance leader and Counselor may post bounties that are open to all alliance members by consuming <b><color=#FF7800>armory resources</color></b>.
- Posting a bounty deducts the required resources from the armory immediately.
<b>2. Claiming rewards</b>
Any alliance member can take on bounties and claim corresponding rewards after completing bounty objectives.
<b>3. Refunding resources</b>
When a bounty is <b><color=#FF7800>canceled, modified, or expires</color></b>, unused armory resources will be automatically returned to the alliance.
<b>4. Resource usage limitations</b>
- For each type of armory resource, the maximum quantity that can be stored at once is <b><color=#FF7800>{p2}</color></b>.
- Armory resources cannot be used for alliance building construction or other purposes.
<b> 5. Weekly reward limits</b>
The maximum amount of Food, Wood, Stone, and Gold that can be obtained each week is <b><color=#FF7800>{p4}</color></b> each, and the maximum for Flux Coins is <b><color=#FF7800>{p5}</color></b>.5
<b><size=18>Posting Bounties</size></b>
The alliance leader and Counselor may post bounties that are open to all alliance members by consuming armory resources.
When posting a bounty, the following conditions must be met:
1. A maximum of <b><color=#FF7800>{p1}</color></b> bounties can exist at the same time.
2. There can only exist <b><color=#FF7800>1</color></b> bounty of each <b><color=#FF7800>bounty type</color></b> at the same time.
3. Each alliance may post a maximum of <b><color=#FF7800>{p2}</color></b> bounties <b><color=#FF7800>every day</color></b>.
4. The <b><color=#FF7800>target count</color></b> for a posted alliance bounty must be commensurate with its <b><color=#FF7800>rewards</color></b>. Otherwise, the bounty cannot be posted.
5. The <b><color=#FF7800>duration</color></b> for each alliance bounty may not last longer than <b><color=#FF7800>{p3}</color></b> hours.
<b><size=18>Modifying or Canceling Bounties</size></b>
1. The alliance leader and Counselor may modify or cancel any bounties that have <b><color=#FF7800>yet to begin</color></b>.
2. Bounties that have begun cannot be modified or canceled.
<b><size=18>Claiming Rewards</size></b>
1. Alliance members may claim bounty rewards immediately upon completing objectives within the bounty's time limit.
2. If more members complete bounties than there are rewards available, rewards will be given according to the order of completion.
3. After bounties expire, members who completed objectives but did not manually claim their rewards will have them sent automatically to the inventory.
4. Repeatable bounties can be taken on multiple times before they expire. Each member can complete them for a maximum of <b><color=#FF7800>{p4}</color></b> times.
