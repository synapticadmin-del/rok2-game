Altars are structures left over from an ancient time, places imbued with amazing power far beyond the imaginations of ordinary men.
1. Altars are open for contending every 3 days. During this period, all alliances are able to attack the altar. Any alliance who can occupy and hold an altar for 4 hours will gain control of it.
2. The first commander (captain of the rallied army) to occupy an altar will become the battle captain. If this commander is withdrawn, the commander with the highest power will automatically succeed the position.
3. Buffs from the same types of altars do not stack.
4. All units who are severely wounded during altar battles will be sent to the hospital.R
Shrines are even more powerful than altars. Once you have captured a shrine, the gods will look down upon you with greater benevolence and you will receive stronger blessings.
1. Shrines are open for contending every 3 days. During this period, all alliances may attack the shrine. Any alliance who can occupy and hold a shrine for 4 hours will gain control of it.
2. The first commander (captain of the rallied army) to occupy a shrine will become the defense captain responsible for repelling enemy attacks. If this commander is withdrawn, the commander with the highest power will automatically succeed the position.
3. Buffs from the same types of shrines do not stack.
4. Half of the units who are severely wounded during shrine battles will die immediately.
5. Shrines can be attacked by rallied armies containing a maximum of 1,500,000 units.

3. Once the initial protection period has ended, passes may be contended at any time.

In addition to season-based divisions, kingdoms in the same season can also be divided into Rising Kingdoms or Imperium Kingdoms based on their kingdom power.
Kingdom Power = The combined individual power of the kingdom's top 300 governors by power. For governors with extremely high power, there are additional calculation rules:
<b>Calculating Kingdom Power Contribution</b>
1. 100% of your power up to 150,000,000 will be factored in to your kingdom's power.
2. 80% of your power from 150,000,000 to 300,000,000 will be factored in to your kingdom's power.
3. 50% of your power from 300,000,000 to 500,000,000 will be factored in to your kingdom's power.
4. Power above 500,000,000 will not factor in to your kingdom's power.
