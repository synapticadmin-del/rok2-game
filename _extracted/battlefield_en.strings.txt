Event Overview
The story begins with the murder of the Lord of Silence, Osiris, at the hands of his covetous brother, Seth. To ensure that Osiris would never return, Seth sealed his body in a coffin and threw it into the Nile. Iset, the wife of Osiris, transformed herself into a bird and flew across Egypt, searching desperately for her husband's body. After much hardship, Iset found what she was seeking. She constructed a temple in which she placed the Ark of Osiris, and began preparing the reincarnation ritual that would restore him to life. Overcome with fury, Seth assembled a mighty army to seize the Ark and put an end to Iset's plan.
Governors will become followers of Seth, fighting to uphold his dominion over the land, or followers of Iset, aiding her in her quest to resurrect the Lord of Silence.
The choices you make and the power you bring to these battlegrounds will decide the fate of these ancient gods and their eternal kingdom.8
After being matched, both alliances will be transported to the Ark of Osiris battle map. One side will be followers of Seth, God of the Desert (Red). The other will be followers of Iset, Goddess of the Sky (Blue).
After the battle begins, you can earn individual points by <color=#EE7600>killing units</color> from the enemy camp. You can earn both individual points and alliance points by <color=#EE7600>occupying buildings</color> or <color=#EE7600>visiting Caravans</color>. Successfully <color=#EE7600>escorting the Ark of Osiris</color> to a building not occupied by the enemy will earn you a ton of individual and alliance points.
At the end of the battle, the side with the most alliance points will be the victor. All governors will receive rewards based on their point totals and whether their alliance won or lost.t
1. Registration
Ark of Osiris is split into two different battlefields. Alliances ranked in their kingdom's Top 20 for alliance power can be registered for the Golden Battlefield by their alliance leader or titled officers. Alliances in the Top 21-100 can participate in the Silver Battlefield. Governors who are part of an alliance when it is registered can only participate with that alliance. Governors who join an alliance after that alliance has registered for Ark of Osiris cannot be selected as combatants, and will not receive corresponding rewards. When registration closes, the list of combatants will be locked, and no further changes will be allowed.
2. Manage Army
Alliance leaders and titled officers can head to the "Manage Army" page and select up to 30 vanguard combatants and 10 backup combatants to participate in the battle. If participating in the Silver Battlefield, at least {p1} alliance members must be selected as combatants to register.
3. Entering the Battlegrounds
The Ark of Osiris battle takes place on a new map separated from the rest of the kingdom. Once the battlegrounds open, governors will need to ensure that all of their troops have returned to the city, no wounded troops are in their hospital, and their city is not in a "War Frenzy" state.
4. The Battle
Upon entering the battlegrounds, the followers of Iset (Blue) will be transported to the upper left corner of the map, while the followers of Seth (Red) will be transported to the bottom right corner.
Preparation Phase: Cannot teleport, occupy structures, or dispatch troops, but alliance markers can be placed.
Battle Phase:
First Period - Obelisks and outposts are unshielded.
Second Period - All remaining structures are unshielded.
Third Period - Ark of Osiris appears.
End Phase: Governors will be automatically sent to the results page. The alliance with the most points will be declared the winner.

Within the Ark of Osiris map, the basic rules of combat are the same as the regular world map. As with the regular map, you can freely move your troops around in Ark of Osiris.
<b>Points</b>
You will earn both alliance and individual points the first time you <color=#EE7600>capture</color> a building (i.e. occupy it until it changes to your camp's color) and by continuing to occupy it. Some buildings (e.g. the Shrine of Life and Shrine of War) will also provide unique buffs while occupied.
<color=#EE7600>Killing</color> enemy troops will earn you individual points, but not alliance points.
You can earn a ton of points by <color=#EE7600>escorting</color> the Ark to a building not occupied by the enemy.
You will only have 15 minutes to escort the Ark, after which the Ark will disappear. It will respawn at the Temple of Osiris after 1 minute.
If you manage to successfully escort the Ark, it will respawn at the Temple of Osiris after some time, and the number of points you can earn by escorting the Ark will increase. If you visit a Caravan while escorting the Ark, the Ark will drop on the map.
<b>Special</b>
Each alliance has a maximum of 20 teleport chances. These teleport chances are <color=#EE7600>shared across your alliance</color> and can be used by any combatant. Your first occupation of an Obelisk will award an additional 8 teleport chances, while reoccupying a previously occupied Obelisk will award 2 teleport chances. While occupied, Obelisks will produce additional teleport chances for your alliance over time.
If your alliance still has teleport chances remaining, you can teleport your city to an Obelisk even if you still have troops outside of your city.
Units <color=#EE7600>cannot be killed</color> in Ark of Osiris battles, but can be wounded and sent to the Hospital. In the Ark of Osiris battle map, your Hospital capacity is unlimited and healing units does not cost resources. All wounded units in the Hospital will <color=#EE7600>automatically recover</color> upon leaving the battle map.
If you fail to defend your War Camp, it will burn very rapidly. If you do not extinguish the fire in time, your War Camp may be forced back to a safe area.
<b>Buffs</b>
1. Buffs from equipment, alliance skills, alliance technologies, alliance titles, individual technologies, commander talents, VIP bonuses, civilization bonuses, city buildings, city themes, and certain items will take effect within the Ark of Osiris battle map.
2. Buffs from formations, armaments, kingdom titles, holy sites, kingdom buffs and runes, as well as king skills will <color=#EE7600>not</color> take effect within the Ark of Osiris battle map.
3. <color=#EE7600>Iconic equipment attributes</color> are based on the Season of the oldest kingdom between the two alliances.

3. This option will not be displayed in situations where you cannot give simultaneous orders.
4. Simultaneous orders cannot be given to rallied armies, carriages, or routed troops.

The Osiris League will serve as the ultimate proving grounds for the world's greatest alliances. League competitors will face off in one head-to-head battle each Sunday, progressing through a series of elimination rounds until only the strongest is left standing!
Stage 1: Qualifiers
A total of 512 alliances will be separated into 32 divisions and will compete in 3 group stage rounds and 2 elimination rounds. Only the winner of each division will receive rank rewards and progress to the next stage.
Stage 2: Playoffs
The 32 division champions will compete in 3 group stage rounds and 4 elimination rounds. At the end of the group stage, only 16 alliances (the top 2 alliances in each group) will progress to the elimination rounds. The winner of the final round will be crowned champion. (Alliance ranking will determine the level of rank rewards received.)
Registration
1. How to Register
After the Osiris League event opens, alliance leaders or officers will need to register their alliance on the event page before the end of the registration period. Competition times and combatants must be selected during registration and cannot be changed once registration ends.
2. Competition Times
All rounds will take place on Sunday. Alliances will need to choose 3 potential competition times from the following time slots and rank them in order of preference (all times in UTC): 03:00, 09:00, 13:00, 15:00, and 19:00. Once matchmaking is complete, the system will automatically determine a competition time based on the times selected by each alliance. (Competition time preferences cannot be changed once the qualifiers have begun.)
3. Participants
Alliance leaders or officers can choose 30 combatants and 15 backups from their alliance to register as participants. Each alliance's power rating will be calculated as the combined power rating of the top 30 governors. This list of participants cannot be changed during qualifiers. (Governors who join an alliance after the end of registration cannot be selected as participants. This restriction will be lifted after the end of qualifiers.)
4. Selection Process
Once registration is complete, the top 512 registered alliances in terms of power rating will be selected to compete in the Osiris League qualifiers. (Alliances who do not make it into the Osiris League will still be able to compete in regular Ark of Osiris battles.)
Qualifiers|
The qualifiers are comprised of 3 group stage rounds and 2 elimination rounds, spanning a total of 5 weeks. Only the top alliance in each division will progress to the playoffs.
1. Divisions
The 512 alliances selected for the Osiris League will be separated into 32 divisions, with 16 alliances in each division. Only the top alliance in each division will progress to the playoffs.
2. Group Stage
The 16 alliances in each division will be further separated into 4 groups (4 alliances in each group) for a round robin stage. At the end of the round robin stage, only the alliance with the most wins in each group will progress to the knockout stage. If multiple alliances within the same group have an equal number of wins, the alliance with the most Net Points from the 3 rounds will move on (Net Points is the point differential between the winning side and the losing side in a single round. The winner earns Net Points and the loser loses Net Points).
3. Knockout Stage
The 4 winners of the group stage will complete in a 2-round single-elimination knockout stage. The winner of each division's knockout stage will progress to the playoffs.
Rest Period
There will be a 7-day rest period following the end of qualifiers. During this period alliance leaders and officers will be able to make certain adjustments to their registration details.
1. Competition Times
During the rest period, alliance leaders and officers will be able to select new competition times and adjust their preferred order. Each alliance will still be required to select 3 potential competition times.
2. Participants
During the rest period, alliance leaders and officers will be able to adjust their alliance's list of participants. Adjustments can be made freely throughout the rest period, but on the final list of participants no more than 10 of the original qualifier stage participants may be removed, no more than 10 new participants may be added, and the total number of participants added must be equal to or greater than the number removed. There is no restriction on the number of times participants can be moved between combatant and substitute roles. Governors included on the list of participants (at the end of registration) for other Division Champions may not be added as new participants (including Division Champions from other Realms), but other newly joined alliance members may be added.
Competition time selections and lists of participants will be locked at the end of the rest period and cannot be changed throughout the rest of the competition. League participant rewards will be sent to the list of participants confirmed at the end of the rest period.
Playoffs%
Playoffs comprise 3 group stage rounds and 4 elimination rounds over 9 weeks. A total of 32 alliances (the champions of the 32 divisions) will enter the playoffs.
1. Group Stage
The 32 alliances who progress to the playoffs will be separated into 8 groups (4 alliances in each group) for a round robin stage. At the end of the round robin stage, the top 2 alliances with the most wins in each group will progress to the knockout stage. If multiple alliances within the same group have an equal number of wins, the alliance with the most Net Points from the 3 rounds will move on. (Net Points is the points difference between the winning side and the losing side in a single round. The winner scores positive Net Points and the loser scores negative Net Points)
2. Knockout Stage
After the group stage, a draw will be made to determine each team's opponents in the Round of 16. The elimination stage will consist of four rounds: Round of 16, Quarterfinals, Semifinals, and Finals. The winner of the final round will be crowned this season's world champion.
3. The Finals
The Finals follows a "best of three" system, which means the winner of the first two rounds will win automatically without the need for a third round. Both sides are still eligible for the third round's "Single Match Victory Reward" regardless.
Only alliances who progress to the playoffs will receive rank rewards. (Rank rewards will be sent within 48 hours of the end of season finals, after the system has verified that no errors occurred.)
Alliance Rewards
All alliance members will receive rewards corresponding to their alliance's performance in the playoffs (governors who join an alliance following the end of the rest period will not receive rank rewards).
Participant Rewards
All Osiris League playoff participants (including substitutes) will receive special rewards corresponding to their alliance's final ranking.
Other Rewards$
While the Osiris League competition is underway, all governors will be able to earn Osiris Coins by participating in Osiris League battles or regular Ark of Osiris battles (including the Golden Battlefield and Silver Battlefield). Osiris Coins can be exchanged for rewards in the League Shop.
Registration Requirements
Alliance
All alliances who have built 10 or more alliance flags can register for the Osiris League.
Individual
All governors with city halls Level 16 or higher can be selected as participants.
Osiris Coins)
While the Osiris League competition is underway, all alliances will receive Osiris Coins after participating in Osiris League battles or regular Ark of Osiris battles (including the Golden Battlefield and Silver Battlefield). All combatants (including substitutes) will receive bonus Osiris Coins.
Special RulesJ
1. All Governors included on the list of participants for an alliance participating in the Osiris League will be unable to participate in regular Ark of Osiris battles until their League alliance has been eliminated from the competition.
2. Governors who have not been included on the list of participants for their alliance may choose to leave their alliance and join another alliance to participate in regular Ark of Osiris battles. However, Governors who claim weekly Ark of Osiris alliance rewards in one alliance will be unable to claim Osiris League alliance rewards in the same week by joining another alliance.
3. Battle mechanics are the same for Osiris League battles and regular Ark of Osiris battles.
4. The alliance and individual rewards received after Osiris League battles are identical to those received following regular Ark of Osiris battles, but the number of Osiris Coins received is different.
5. If point scores are tied at the end of the match, the alliance that registered with the highest total power will be declared the winner.
6. Alliances participating in the Qualifiers will occasionally go through to the next round with an automatic victory if the system is unable to match them to a suitable opponent, e.g. if there is an odd number of participating alliances.
7. Alliance members who have already been entered onto the list of participants may not change alliances during the rest period before their current alliance has been eliminated. If a Governor's alliance has been eliminated, they can join another alliance which has also advanced to the Playoffs in the rest period.

1) In the event of a critical issue or server instability that impacts the fairness of a match, League officials reserve the right to suspend the match at any time. Whether or not an issue affects the fairness of a match is up to the sole discretion of the League officials.
2) Critical issues may include but are not limited to: server instability, game-breaking bugs, serious abuse of loopholes or design flaws, cheating through illegal programs, etc.
3) If a match was not suspended, the results of the match will be deemed final, with the following exception:
If a match was not suspended despite the occurrence of a critical issue, League officials reserve the right to schedule a redo, depending on the severity of the issue. See "Redo Policy" for more details.
4) Alliance leaders are the sole representatives of their teams, and the liaisons between the League officials and their teams. All official communication is conducted through alliance leaders.
5) Issues and disputes must be reported to customer support via your alliance leader. Please provide a detailed description along with evidence. Contact information for customer support has already been sent to alliance leaders.

7) In the event of a critical issue, League officials will send further notification via in-game mail within 48 hours. If there is any conflict or ambiguity between communication channels, the content of in-game mail will prevail.
8) Participants are expected to adhere to the spirit of fair play. Intentionally throwing or fixing a match for any reason will be penalized according to the severity of the offense. 1. In the Ark of Osiris, your alliance leaders and titled officers can set up skills by tapping on <color=#EE7600>skill icons</color> in the Management panel.
2. Your alliance can equip up to <b>6</b> different skills in each Ark of Osiris match.

4. Each skill has its own <color=#EE7600>cooldown</color>. Some skills are so powerful that you can only use them once per match.
5. Energy Point recovery rate depends on the <color=#EE7600>difference between both alliances' scores</color>. The alliance with the lower score will gain Energy Points faster. The bigger the difference between both alliances' scores, the faster the alliance with the lower score will gain Energy Points.
6. Only <color=#EE7600>alliance leaders and titled officers</color> can use skills. Use them wisely, for an expertly-timed skill can turn the battle in your favor.
7. The effects of the Running Charge, War Drums, and Mystical Formation skills will be <color=#EE7600>removed immediately</color> after entering a Stronghold.

Introduction
ABOUTI
The Osiris League may be over, but the passion for battle and competition remains.
We will be inviting the champion alliances of the four realms to compete in one more competition, a chance to show their might in a glorious contest of the elite!
Who will honor Osiris through domination on the battlefield? Only time will tell.
Gameplay
COMMANDERS
1. Governors will be unable to use their own commanders during the Grand Finals. The system will provide designated commanders for each governor to use until the end of the competition.
2. Commanders provided will include advanced, elite, epic, and legendary commanders.
3. The commander levels and abilities of all provided commanders will be at the maximum level, and epic and legendary commanders will be Expertise attained.
4. Talents will have to be selected manually by governors, and can be reset unlimited times for free, but cannot be reset while a match is in progress.
5. Use of equipment will not be allowed.
6. Commanders provided can only be used in the Grand Finals, and cannot be used in any other part of the game.
BUFFS6
1. Within the Grand Finals, technology, VIP levels, and alliance technology buffs will not take effect. Instead, all governors will receive the same set of buffs.
2. All other buffs will not take effect either, including those from kingdom titles, Crystal technologies, the occupation of Holy Sites, and so on.
TROOPSL
1. Governors will be unable to use their own troops during the Grand Finals. The system will provide each governor with 4,000,000 Tier 5 units of each troop type from their respective civilization, as well as 500,000 Tier 1 cavalry units.
2. These troops can only be viewed and used within the Grand Finals, and will be only last until the end of the tournament.
3. Governors can heal provided troops the same way as regular troops.
4. After the Preparation Phase, governors can tap the "Troop Management" button on the Grand Finals page to get an early start on configuring their troops.
Rewards
REWARDST
1. At the end of the Grand Finals, commemorative prizes will be distributed according to the competition results.
2. Only participating governors will receive commemorative rewards after each match.
3. Participating governors will also receive individual rewards based on match results, and earn additional rewards for their whole alliance.

A realm will not be considered to have entered the Season of Conquest until all kingdoms of participating alliances have done so.
Realm Status Determination Times
The system will determine the status of a realm first at the beginning of registration, and then again at the beginning of the rest period.
Range of Determination
Realm status determination will only take into account data from kingdoms whose alliances have not been eliminated.

In order to ensure fair competition, each realm of the Osiris League will have specific restrictions on which commanders can be used.

Schedule Overviewc
The Sunset Canyon Tournament is for kingdoms that are in the <b>Preparation Season</b>. Governors can enter combat in the Sunset Canyon across 4 kingdoms in the same region.
Each season of the Tournament starts on a Monday and lasts for 3 weeks, including 1 week for the preselection stage, 1 week for the main stage, and 1 week for the champion showcase.
Preselection#
During this stage, in addition to regular rewards, governors will have a chance to qualify for the Tournament when they participate in the <b>Sunset Canyon</b> event in their kingdom.
<b>Kingdom Breakout</b>
Governors can view their ranks and <b>total points</b> in real time for 6 days. The <b>total points</b> is the sum of governors' daily Sunset Canyon <b>tallied points</b> during the Kingdom Breakout period (6 days). Before points are tallied for the day, they will be updated in real time.
If multiple governors have the same score, they will be ranked based on how quickly they reached that score.
If a governor immigrates during this period, they will be disqualified in their original kingdom, and the total points will be cleared. If a Governor immigrates to a kingdom during the Kingdom Breakout period, their total points and rank in the new kingdom will be calculated starting from the day they immigrated.
<b>Qualifier Showcase</b>
Lasts 1 day. The top <color=#a24000><b>32</b></color> governors with the most <b>total points</b> during the Kingdom Breakout phase will automatically qualify for the Tournament without having to register.
If a qualified governor immigrates during the qualifier showcase period or subsequent stages, regardless of the target kingdom, they will be <b>disqualified</b>.
Main Event~
<b>Group Stage</b>
Lasts for 1 day and is a round-robin tournament.
128 governors from 4 kingdoms will be assigned to 8 <b>divisions</b>. There will be 4 <b>groups</b> of 4 governors in each division. The system will attempt to group governors from different kingdoms.
The governors will compete with the other governors in their group one by one. For each match, the winner will get 1 point, and the loser will get no points. The top 2 governors in each group will qualify for the qualifying stage. 8 governors from each division will enter the qualifying stage. If there are governors with the same number of points in a group, troop power (not the governors' power) will act as a tiebreaker.
<b>Qualifying Stage</b>
Lasts for 3 days and is a single-elimination tournament.
The 8 qualifying governors in each division will be randomly matched, and one round of the single-elimination tournament will be held every day. The winner will qualify for the next round, and the loser will be eliminated. The last governor standing will be the <b>division champion</b>.
<b>Championship</b>
Lasts for 3 days and is a single-elimination tournament.
The 8 division champions will be randomly matched, and one round of the single-elimination tournament will be held every day. The winner will qualify for the next round, and the loser will be eliminated. The last governor standing will be the <b>final champion</b>.
The Competition
Troop Settings
1. During the competition, before <color=#a24000><b>23:00 UTC</b></color> every day, you can adjust your troops for the <b>current round</b>. After that, the troops will be <b>locked</b> and cannot be adjusted.
2. Before troops are locked, all buffs will be <b>automatically synchronized</b> to the participating troops. Please avoid anything that would reduce the power of your troops, such as removing equipment.
3. You view other governors' battle records, but you can't view their current troops.
4. If you don't set your troops in a round, your troop settings from the <b>previous round</b> will be used by default (if you don't set your troops in the Group Stage, your troop settings will be based on your defensive troop settings at the <b>end</b> of the Kingdom Breakout stage).
5. System maintenance may affect your ability to adjust your troops. Please pay attention to system announcements, and try to avoid adjusting your troops during system maintenance periods.
Competition Results
1. During the competition phase, at <color=#a24000><b>24:00 UTC</b></color> every day, governors' deployed troops will automatically compete with other governors' troops.
2. The competition results will be sent by mail to all the governors participating in the current round, and updated on the Schedule interface. Mail may be delayed, so please wait patiently or contact customer service.
Battle Rules
Tournament combat rules are the same as Sunset Canyon:
1. Each governor may deploy a maximum of 5 troops. <b>The configuration of all troops will be determined automatically</b>.
2. Governors must place their troops in one of 8 squares. Each troop will first attack the front-row targets directly in front of them, followed by the back-row targets directly in front of them. If there are no targets directly in front, the troop will attack the closest target.
3. Only buffs from <b>commander levels, talents, equipment and the VIP troop capacity boost</b> will take effect in battles.
4. Defeat all enemy troops within the time limit to claim victory. If neither side has been eliminated by the end of the time limit, the side with the most remaining troops will be the winner.

As Seth and Iset wake from their slumber, the sands of Egypt have begun to stir...
Welcome to Ark of Osiris: Raging Sands, where you will need to fight through the sands themselves if you wish to control Egypt.
Ark of Osiris: Raging Sands Battlefield Overview:
1. The Sandstorm: A raging Sandstorm will cover the entire map, acting as a "fog of war" and limiting your view of the battlefield. New skills and building buffs will help you navigate the harsh sands.
2. Shu's Favor: Shu, God of Wind and He Who Rises Up, has seen fit to grant you his blessing. At the start of battle, all troops on both sides will receive Shu's Favor, increasing their March Speed to help them get to the battlefield more quickly. Some skills that increase March Speed cannot be used while you have Shu's Favor.
<b><size=24>Event Registration</size></b>
Ark of Osiris is divided into the <color=#EE7600>Golden Battlefield</color> and the <color=#EE7600>Silver Battlefield</color>. The top 20 alliances on your kingdom's power rankings can participate in the Golden Battlefield. The 21st to 100th alliances can participate in the Silver Battlefield.
<b>Alliances</b>
Before registration begins, alliances planning to participate in the Golden or Silver Battlefields must have at least 10 <color=#EE7600>Alliance Flags</color>. An alliance must be in the top 20 of their <color=#EE7600>kingdom's power rankings</color> to participate in the Golden Battlefield.
<b>Individuals</b>
To take part in the Ark of Osiris, you must have joined your alliance and upgraded your City Hall to <color=#EE7600>Level 16 or higher</color> before your alliance registered for the event. If you do not meet these requirements, you cannot be chosen as a combatant. 
<b>How to Register</b>
Once registration opens, the <color=#EE7600>alliance leaders or titled officers</color> of qualified alliances can register directly on the event page. Alliances who have not met registration requirements when registration opens will be unable to participate in that instance of the event.

<b><size=24>Rules</size></b>
1. Requirements
Currently, only official admins can create matches. If you receive an <color=#EE7600>invite code</color> for a match, you can enter the <color=#EE7600>invite code</color> to join the match.
2. Participant Statuses
Admins can set participating governors as captains, vanguards, or spectators.
<b>Captains</b>: Each team can have <color=#EE7600>1</color> captain. Captains can modify their team name, configure and use team skills, manage their team's members, and choose their team's vanguards. Captains will spawn at the front of their team's safe zone when entering the battlefield.
<b>Vanguards</b>: Each team can have <color=#EE7600>2</color> vanguards. Vanguards can change their team's name, configure team skills, and use team skills. Vanguards will spawn at the front of their team's safe zone when entering the battlefield.
Spectators: Spectators can watch a match after it starts.
3. Match Settings
Only admins can edit match settings, including the match name, usable commanders, and commander levels (see below for more details).
In addition, only admins are able to lock/unlock match settings or start the match:
 - Locking/Unlocking: Admins can lock or unlock match-related settings. While a match is locked, its settings cannot be changed and new governors cannot join.
 - Starting the Match: Matches must be <color=#EE7600>manually</color> started by an admin.
4. Commander Settings
 - Usable Commanders: Admins can select a list of usable commanders for a match. Commanders <color=#EE7600>not on the list</color> cannot be used in that match.
 - Commander Level Modes: Admins can select between "actual" or "balanced" mode, which will affect the level of all commanders in the match.
   <b>Actual</b>: This mode will use each governor's <color=#EE7600>actual data</color>, including your commander levels, equipment, items, city theme, technologies, VIP level, and unit. All governors will also receive the effects of all alliance technologies at <color=#EE7600>max level</color>.
   <b>Balanced</b>: In this mode, all governors will be provided the same commanders (you can configure your commanders in the commander settings page), equipment, items, and units. All governors will also receive the effects of all technologies, VIP level bonuses, alliance technologies, and buildings at <color=#EE7600>max level</color>. City themes will have no effect in this mode.
 - Commander Settings: In balanced mode, you can head to the commander settings page to configure your commanders' talents, equipment, and other settings. You can still configure your commanders even if the match's settings have been locked.
5. Other Rules
 - If a custom Ark of Osiris match coincides with another Ark of Osiris match, you must select which one to join. You cannot join another Ark of Osiris match before the one you are currently in ends.
 - If an admin does not start a match within 7 days after it is created, it will be automatically canceled.L

<b><size=24>Event Rules</size></b>
1. Once the RoK Global Champions Grand Prix begins, you will be able to place bets each week from Monday to Friday UTC. Betting is divided into "Set Bets" and "Single Bets".
2. Set Bets allow you to bet on all matches across the entire tournament, but you can only bet on one side in any given match. Once you've made a bet, you can increase your wager but cannot withdraw it. In addition to their original bet, governors who bet on the winner of a match will receive a portion (based on the size of their original bet) of the total amount bet by governors who bet on the loser. Governors who bet on the loser will be refunded 50% of their original bet.
3. Single Bets allow you to bet on a single match in each round of the tournament. You can bet up to 200 Grand Aurics per round, and you cannot increase your wager after placing your bet. Governors who bet on the winner will receive 2x their original bet once the match ends; governors who bet on the loser will not receive any Grand Aurics back. In the case of a draw, all Grand Aurics will be refunded.
4. Any Grand Aurics you win will be verified and sent to you via mail after each match ends.
5. Grand Aurics can only be earned from the Grand Prix Betting and Allure of Gold events.
6. At the end of the event, all unused Grand Aurics will be converted into Food at a 1:1 ratio.

Tides of War
Within the Ark of Osiris map, the basic rules of combat are the same as the regular world map. As with the regular map, you can freely move your troops around in Ark of Osiris.
<b>Points</b>
You will earn both alliance and individual points the first time you <color=#EE7600>capture</color> a building (i.e. occupy it until it changes to your camp's color) and by continuing to occupy it. Some buildings (e.g. the Shrine of Life and Shrine of War) will also provide unique buffs while occupied.
<color=#EE7600>Killing</color> enemy troops will earn you individual points, but not alliance points.
You can earn a ton of points by <color=#EE7600>escorting</color> the Ark to a building not occupied by the enemy.
You will only have 15 minutes to escort the Ark, after which the Ark will disappear. It will respawn at the Temple of Osiris after 1 minute.
If you manage to successfully escort the Ark, it will respawn at the Temple of Osiris after some time, and the number of points you can earn by escorting the Ark will increase. If you visit a Caravan while escorting the Ark, the Ark will drop on the map.
<b>Special</b>
Each alliance has a maximum of 20 teleport chances. These teleport chances are <color=#EE7600>shared across your alliance</color> and can be used by any combatant. Your first occupation of an Obelisk will earn your alliance an additional 8 teleport chances, while reoccupying a previously occupied Obelisk will earn 2 teleport chances. While occupied, Obelisks will produce additional teleport chances for your alliance over time.
If your alliance still has teleport chances remaining, you can teleport your War Camp to an Obelisk even if you still have troops outside of your city.
Units <color=#EE7600>cannot be killed</color> in Ark of Osiris battles, but can be wounded and sent to the Hospital. In the Ark of Osiris battle map, your Hospital capacity is unlimited and healing units does not cost resources. All wounded units in the Hospital will <color=#EE7600>automatically recover</color> upon leaving the battle map.
If you fail to defend your War Camp, it will burn very rapidly. If you do not extinguish the fire in time, your city may be forced back to a safe area.
<b>Buffs</b>
1. Buffs from equipment, formations, armaments, alliance skills, alliance technologies, alliance titles, individual technologies, commander talents, VIP bonuses, civilization bonuses, city buildings, city themes, and certain items will take effect within the Ark of Osiris battle map.
2. Buffs from kingdom titles, holy sites, kingdom buffs and runes as well as king skills <color=#EE7600>will not</color> take effect within the battlegrounds.
3. <color=#EE7600>Iconic equipment attributes</color> are based on the Season of the oldest kingdom between the two alliances.

After the group stage, a draw will be held to determine each team's opponents in the Round of 16. The elimination stage will consist of four rounds: Round of 16, Quarterfinals, Semifinals, and Finals. The winner of the final round will be crowned this season's world champion.
If two or more alliances from the same kingdom reach the Round of 16, those alliances will be assigned to the same half of the bracket (if possible).
3. The Finals

Only admins can edit match-wide settings, including the match name, usable commanders, and its commander level mode (see below for more details).

 - Locking/Unlocking: Admins can lock or unlock match-related settings. While a match is locked, its settings cannot be changed and new governors cannot join.
 - Starting Matches: Matches must be <color=#c14d00>manually</color> started by an admin.
 - Cancelling Matches: Admins can <color=#c14d00>manually</color> cancel and close the match.

   <b>Balanced</b>: In this mode, all governors will be provided the same commanders, equipment, items, and units. 1. How to Register
Once the Osiris League event opens, the alliance leader, counselor, warlord, envoy, and saint must register their alliance on the event page <color=#EE7600>before the end of the registration period</color>. They must also select their alliance's <color=#EE7600>match times</color> and <color=#EE7600>participant list</color>. The participant list must have at least 15 governors and include at least 1 coordinator.
This info cannot be changed after the end of registration.
2. Match Times
All rounds will take place on Sunday. During registration, alliances will need to choose 3 potential match times from the following time slots and rank them in order of preference: 03:00, 09:00, 13:00, 15:00, and 19:00 UTC. Once matchmaking is complete, matches will be assigned a time based on the time slots selected by both alliances.
Match time preferences cannot be changed during the qualifiers.
3. Participants
The alliance leader, counselor, warlord, envoy, and saint can select <color=#EE7600>30 starters</color> and <color=#EE7600>15 substitutes</color> from their alliance for the Osiris League.
An alliance's <color=#EE7600>total power</color> is the sum of the power of the 30 alliance members with the highest individual power.
Participant lists cannot be changed during the qualifiers, though starters and substitutes can be swapped on non-match days. This restriction will be lifted during the rest period.
4. Selection Process
Once registration is complete, only the <color=#EE7600>top 512</color> registered alliances in terms of total power will be selected to compete in the Osiris League qualifiers.
Alliances that aren't selected can still compete in regular Ark of Osiris matches.
1. If you are on the participant list for an alliance competing in the Osiris League, you will be unable to compete in regular Ark of Osiris battles until your alliance has been eliminated from the competition.
2. If you aren't selected for your alliance's Osiris League participant list, you can choose to join a different alliance and compete in regular Ark of Osiris battles. However, you can only claim <color=#EE7600>1 alliance reward</color>. If you claim your new alliance's weekly Ark of Osiris rewards, then rejoin your old alliance, you won't be able to claim that week's Osiris League alliance rewards.
3. Battle mechanics are the same for Osiris League battles and regular Ark of Osiris battles.
4. The alliance and individual rewards for Osiris League battles are identical to the rewards for regular Ark of Osiris battles, other than a different number of Osiris Coins.
5. If a match ends with a tied score, the alliance with the highest total power at time of registration will be declared the winner. However, if both alliances end the match with a score of 0, they will both be declared losers and neither will receive any group stage points.
6. During the qualifiers, the system may occasionally fail to find a suitable opponent for an alliance (e.g. there is an odd number of participating alliances). If that happens, the alliance will receive a bye and automatically progress to the next round.
7. If you are on your alliance's list of Osiris League participants and your alliance has not been eliminated, you cannot change alliances during the rest period. If your alliance has been eliminated, you can join an alliance that has advanced to the playoffs during the rest period.
8. If a governor on an alliance's participant list immigrates during the Osiris League, they will be <color=#EE7600>removed from the alliance's participant list</color> and will be unable to receive Osiris League alliance rewards and individual rewards from the alliance.
The playoffs are comprised of <color=#EE7600>3 group stage rounds</color> and <color=#EE7600>4 knockout rounds</color>, spanning a total of 9 weeks. A total of 32 alliances (the champions of the 32 divisions) will enter the playoffs.
1. Group Stage
The 32 alliances will be split into 8 groups of 4 and compete against every other alliance in their group. Wins grant 1 point, losses grant none.
After 3 rounds, the top 2 alliances in each group will advance to the knockout stage. The others will be eliminated.
If two alliances in the same group are tied in points, the alliance with the most Net Points will be ranked higher (Net Points refers to the point differential between the winner and loser of each match. The winner of a match gains Net Points, the loser loses Net Points). 
2. Knockout Stage
The knockout stage is comprised of the Round of 16, Quarterfinals, Semifinals, and Finals. In knockout stage matches, winners advance to the next round, losers are eliminated.
At the end of the group stage, a draw will be held to decide the matchups for the Round of 16.
The winner of the knockout stage will be crowned the champion of the Osiris League!

There will be a <color=#EE7600>1-day rest period</color> following the end of the Semifinals. - City Hall below level {p6}.

1. Creating a Match and Inviting Participants
Any governor can create a custom Ark of Osiris match.
Once you've successfully created a custom match, you will have 14 days to start the match or it will automatically be canceled. You will be automatically set as an admin for the match and will receive an <color=#c14d00>invite code</color>.
Other governors can use your <color=#c14d00>invite code</color> to join your match.
2. Participant Statuses
Admins can set other governors in the match as combatants or spectators. They can also set the lineups of both teams and assign team captains/vanguards.
<b>Captains</b>: Each team can have <color=#c14d00>1</color> captain. Captains can modify their team name, configure and use team skills, manage their team's members, choose their team's vanguards, and assign battlefield roles to their team members. Captains will spawn at the front of their team's safe zone when entering the battlefield.
<b>Vanguards</b>: Each team can have <color=#c14d00>4</color> vanguards. Vanguards can change their team's name, configure and use team skills, and assign battlefield roles to their team members. When entering the battlefield, the two vanguards on each team with the highest matchmaking scores will spawn at the front of their team's safe zone.
Spectators: Spectators can watch a match after it starts.
3. Match Settings
Only admins can edit match-wide settings, including the match name, usable commanders, and its commander level mode (see below for more details).
In addition, only admins are able to lock/unlock match settings or start the match:
 - Locking/Unlocking: Admins can lock or unlock match-related settings. While a match is locked, its settings cannot be changed and new governors cannot join.
 - Starting Matches: Matches must be <color=#c14d00>manually</color> started by an admin.
 - Cancelling Matches: Admins can <color=#c14d00>manually</color> cancel and close the match.
4. Commander Settings
 - Usable Commanders: Admins can select a list of usable commanders for the match. Commanders not on the list <color=#c14d00>cannot be used in the match</color>.
 - Commander Level Modes: Admins can select between "actual" or "balanced" mode, which will affect the level of all commanders in the match.
   <b>Actual</b>: This mode will use each governor's <color=#c14d00>actual data</color>, including your commander levels, equipment, items, city themes, technologies, VIP level, units, etc. All governors will also receive the effects of all alliance technologies at <color=#c14d00>max level</color>.
   <b>Balanced</b>: In this mode, all governors will be provided the same commanders, equipment, items, and units. All governors will also receive the effects of all technologies, VIP level bonuses, alliance technologies, and buildings at <color=#c14d00>max level</color>. City themes will have no effect in this mode. In addition, you <color=#c14d00>cannot heal</color> wounded units in this mode. You can configure your commanders in the commander settings page, but you can only use commanders that you own and are available in your kingdom's current Season.
 - Commander Settings: In balanced mode, you can head to the commander settings page to configure your commanders' talents, equipment, and other settings. You can still configure your commanders even if the match's settings have been locked.
5. Other Buff Settings
 - <b>Army Expansion</b>: Admins can choose how much to increase troop unit capacity.
 - <b>Lost Kingdom Buffs</b>: Admins can grant all participants buffs from the Past Glory event and Crystal technologies.
6. Other
 - If a custom Ark of Osiris match coincides with another Ark of Osiris match, you must select which one to join. You cannot join another Ark of Osiris match before the one you are currently in ends.
 - If you do not start a match you created within 14 days of creating it, it will be automatically canceled and the costs of the match will not be refunded.

 <b>Entering the Battle Map</b>

 <b>Battlefield Roles</b>
Ark of Osiris combatants can be assigned one of three roles: Rally, Garrison, or Field. While the outcome of each match will still be based on your personal initiative and tactics, your assigned role will help guide your priorities and align you with the rest of your team.
<b>Rally</b>: Tasked with initiating rallies against non-allied buildings and capturing as many buildings as possible.
<b>Garrison</b>: Tasked with garrisoning allied buildings and holding the line against enemy attacks.
<b>Field</b>: Tasked with dispatching troops to join rallies and garrisons, as well as fighting enemy troops on the map.

The qualifiers are comprised of 3 group stage rounds and 2 elimination rounds, spanning a total of 5 weeks. Only the top alliance in each division will progress to the playoffs.
1. Divisions
The 512 alliances selected for the Osiris League will be separated into 32 divisions, with 16 alliances in each division. Only the top alliance in each division will progress to the playoffs.
2. Group Stage
The 16 alliances in each division will be further separated into 4 groups (4 alliances in each group) for a round robin stage. At the end of the round robin stage, only the alliance with the most wins in each group will progress to the knockout stage. If multiple alliances within the same group have an equal number of wins, the alliance with the most Net Points from the 3 rounds will move on (Net Points is the point differential between the winning side and the losing side in a single round. The winner earns Net Points and the loser loses Net Points).
3. Knockout Stage
The 4 winners of the group stage will complete in a 2-round single-elimination knockout stage. The winner of each division's knockout stage will progress to the playoffs.

1. Creating a Match and Inviting Participants
Any governor can create a custom Ark of Osiris match.
Once you've successfully created a custom match, you will have 14 days to start the match or it will automatically be canceled. You will be automatically set as a match admin and will receive an <color=#c14d00>invite code</color>.
Other governors can use your <color=#c14d00>invite code</color> to join your match.
2. Participant Statuses
Admins can set other governors in the match as combatants or spectators. They can also set the lineups of both teams and assign team captains/vanguards.
<b>Captains</b>: Each team can have <color=#c14d00>1</color> captain. Captains can modify their team name, configure and use team skills, manage their team's members, choose their team's vanguards, and assign battlefield roles to their team members. Captains will spawn at the front of their team's safe zone when entering the battlefield.
<b>Vanguards</b>: Each team can have <color=#c14d00>4</color> vanguards. Vanguards can change their team's name, configure and use team skills, and assign battlefield roles to their team members. When entering the battlefield, the two vanguards on each team with the highest matchmaking scores will spawn at the front of their team's safe zone.
Spectators: Spectators can watch a match after it starts.
3. Match Settings
Only admins can edit match-wide settings, including the match name, usable commanders, and its commander level mode (see below for more details).
In addition, only admins are able to lock/unlock match settings or start the match:
 - Locking/Unlocking: Admins can lock or unlock match-related settings. While a match is locked, its settings cannot be changed and new governors cannot join.
 - Starting Matches: Matches must be <color=#c14d00>manually</color> started by an admin.
 - Cancelling Matches: Admins can <color=#c14d00>manually</color> cancel and close the match.
4. Commander Settings
 - Usable Commanders: Admins can select a list of usable commanders for the match. Commanders not on the list <color=#c14d00>cannot be used in the match</color>.
 - Commander Level Modes: Admins can select between "actual" or "balanced" mode, which will affect the level of all commanders in the match.
   <b>Actual</b>: This mode will use each governor's <color=#c14d00>actual data</color>, including your commander levels, equipment, items, city themes, technologies, VIP level, units, etc. All governors will also receive the effects of all alliance technologies at <color=#c14d00>max level</color>.
   <b>Balanced</b>: In this mode, all governors will be provided the same commanders, equipment, items, and units. All governors will also receive the effects of all technologies, VIP level bonuses, alliance technologies, and buildings at <color=#c14d00>max level</color>. City themes will have no effect in this mode. In addition, you <color=#c14d00>cannot heal</color> wounded units in this mode. You can configure your commanders in the commander settings page, but you can only use commanders that you own and are available in your kingdom's current Season.
 - Commander Settings: In balanced mode, you can head to the commander settings page to configure your commanders' talents, equipment, and other settings. You can still configure your commanders even if the match's settings have been locked.
 - Iconic Equipment Attributes: In Balanced Mode, iconic equipment attributes will be based on the season of the oldest kingdom among the two teams.
5. Other Buff Settings
 - <b>Army Expansion</b>: Admins can choose how much to increase troop unit capacity.
 - <b>Lost Kingdom Buffs</b>: Admins can grant all participants buffs from the Past Glory event and Crystal technologies.
6. Other
 - If a custom Ark of Osiris match coincides with another Ark of Osiris match, you must select which one to join. You cannot join another Ark of Osiris match before the one you are currently in ends.
 - If you do not start a match you created within 14 days of creating it, it will be automatically canceled and the costs of the match will not be refunded.
 - Anubis's strength during battle is determined by the season of the match creator's kingdom.

There will be a <color=#EE7600>7-day rest period</color> after the qualifiers and a <color=#EE7600>1-day rest period</color> after the Semifinals.
During these rest periods, alliance leaders and officers can make a number of adjustments:
1. Match Times
During either rest period, alliance leaders and officers will be able to change their match time preferences. Every alliance will still be required to select 3 potential match times.
2. Participants
During the rest period after the qualifiers:
- Alliance leaders and officers can adjust their list of participants. On the final list of participants, <color=#EE7600>the total number of participants added or removed cannot exceed 15</color>. There is no limit on how many times participants can be swapped between starter and substitute.
- During this period, newly-joined alliance members can be added to the participant list, with the exception of governors that were on the list of participants (at the end of registration) for another division champion (including division champions from other realms).
- At the end of this rest period, participant lists will be locked in for the rest of the competition. League participant rewards will be sent to the participant list confirmed at the end of the rest period.
- If a governor on an alliance's participant list immigrates during the Osiris League while their alliance is still competing, they will be <color=#EE7600>removed from the alliance's participant list</color> and will be unable to receive alliance or individual rewards from the alliance.
During the rest period after the Semifinals:
- Participant lists cannot be changed.
- If you participate in the Ark of Osiris during the rest period, you can no longer be added to the participant list.
