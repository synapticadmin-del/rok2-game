Attack March
The attack march command can be enabled from <color=#fff117>Settings > Combat Settings</color>. To order an attack march, select an empty spot on the map to open the interaction bubble, then select the Attack March button.
A troop ordered to attack march will begin marching to the target location. Along the way, they will <color=#fff117>automatically pursue and attack</color> any enemy troops they encounter within a certain range.
Attack
You can order your troops to attack NPC enemies and governors that aren't in your alliance. If you order a troop to attack another troop, your troop will automatically pursue the target.
Gather
You can dispatch a troop to a <color=#fff117>resource point</color> to gather resources over time. You'll receive the resources the troop has gathered once it returns to your city.
Teleport
You can use a <color=#fff117>teleport item</color> to teleport your city to another location on the map (the specific location depends on the type of teleport item). You cannot teleport while fighting against a rallied army.
Rallies
Rallying allows you and your fellow alliance members to challenge powerful enemies by combining multiple troops into a single "rallied army". <color=#fff117>Troops in a rallied army will only receive commander effects and buffs from the rally captain's troop</color>.
You can initiate rallies against Holy Sites, Passes, enemy cities, Barbarian Forts, and other targets. If no allied troops join a rally by the time its countdown ends, the rally will be canceled.
You can join ongoing rallies via the Alliance War interface.
Garrisoning
You can dispatch troops to garrison allied cities, Alliance Flags, Passes, strongholds, and other targets. <color=#fff117>Any garrisoned troops will only receive the commander effects and buffs of the garrison captain's troop</color>.
Construction Assistance
You can dispatch troops to help build unfinished alliance strongholds. The more troops in a stronghold, the faster it'll be built.
Melee and Ranged CombatB
Generally speaking, a troop can only be switched to <color=#fff117>ranged mode</color> if its commander is using the V formation. Certain Lost Kingdom stories have special rules regarding switching to ranged mode. Troops in ranged mode <color=#fff117>deal ranged basic attack damage</color> instead of basic attack damage.
Normal Damageh
Normal damage includes basic attack damage, counterattack damage, combo attack damage, and smite damage.
Ranged normal damage includes ranged basic attack damage, ranged counterattack damage, ranged combo attack damage, and ranged smite damage.+
Basic Attack and Ranged Basic Attack Damage
Once per second, melee troops will launch a basic attack against their <color=#fff117>primary target</color>. Basic attack damage numbers are displayed in white on the map.
Once per second, ranged troops will launch a ranged basic attack against their <color=#fff117>primary target</color>. Ranged basic attack damage numbers are displayed in white on the map.
Counterattack Damage
Whenever a troop is hit with a basic attack, it will automatically <color=#fff117>retaliate</color> with a counterattack. Counterattack damage numbers are displayed in white on the map.
Whenever a troop is hit with a ranged basic attack, it will automatically <color=#fff117>retaliate</color> with a ranged counterattack. Ranged counterattack damage numbers are displayed in white on the map.
Combo Attack Damage
Combo attack damage is only affected by bonuses/reductions to normal damage. <color=#fff117>Combo attacks can trigger basic attack skills</color>, but they <color=#fff117>will not trigger counterattacks</color>. Combo attack damage numbers are displayed in red on the map.:
Ranged combo attack damage is only affected by bonuses/reductions to ranged normal damage. <color=#fff117>Ranged combo attacks can trigger ranged basic attack skills</color>, but they <color=#fff117>will not trigger ranged counterattacks</color>. Ranged combo attack damage numbers are displayed in red on the map.
Smite Damage
Smite damage is a type of normal damage. It is affected by bonuses/reductions to normal damage rather than skill damage. Smite damage numbers are displayed in red on the map.
Ranged smite damage is a type of ranged normal damage. It is affected by bonuses/reductions to ranged normal damage rather than ranged skill damage. Ranged smite damage numbers are displayed in red on the map.
Skill Damage:
Skill damage includes direct damage and additional damage.O
Ranged skill damage includes ranged direct damage and ranged additional damage.
Direct Damage_
Direct damage is a type of skill damage. Direct damage numbers are displayed in red on the map.t
Ranged direct damage is a type of ranged skill damage. Ranged direct damage numbers are displayed in red on the map.
Additional Damage
Additional damage is a type of skill damage. It is typically used for effects that deal damage <color=#fff117>over time</color>. These effects will deal additional damage every second for the duration of the effect. Additional damage numbers are displayed in red on the map..
Ranged additional damage is a type of ranged skill damage. It is typically used for effects that deal damage <color=#fff117>over time</color>. These effects will deal ranged additional damage every second for the duration of the effect. Ranged additional damage numbers are displayed in red on the map.
True Damagem
True damage ignores the attacking troop's Attack and the defending troop's Defense during damage calculation.t
Ranged true damage ignores the attacking troop's Attack and the defending troop's Defense during damage calculation.
Ranged Damage*
Ranged damage is a special category of damage, calculated differently from non-ranged damage. Troops in ranged mode will deal ranged basic attack damage instead of basic attack damage. Troops hit by ranged basic attacks will respond with ranged counterattack damage instead of counterattack damage.
Normal Buffs+
Normal buffs include effects that:
- Increase the target's Attack, Defense, or Health
- Increase the target's damage dealt or reduce its damage received
- Increase the effectiveness of shields/healing the target grants/receives
Normal buffs can be <color=#fff117>dispelled</color> by certain skills.
Normal Debuff*
Normal debuffs include effects that:
- Reduce the target's Attack, Defense, or Health
- Reduce the target's damage dealt or increase its damage received
- Reduce the effectiveness of shields/healing the target grants/receives
Normal debuffs can be <color=#fff117>cleansed</color> by certain skills.
Control EffectsA
Control effects include Silence, Disarmed, and Heal Immune.
<color=#fff117>Silence</color>: Target <color=#fff117>cannot use active skills</color>.
<color=#fff117>Disarm</color>: Target <color=#fff117>cannot launch basic attacks</color>.
<color=#fff117>Heal Immune</color>: Target <color=#fff117>cannot be healed</color>.
Special Buffs and Debuffs
Some commander skills have unique buff/debuff effects, such as Zhuge Liang's "Marquis". These effects have unique names and <color=#fff117>cannot be cleansed or dispelled</color>.
Cleanse6
Cleanse effects remove normal debuffs from the target.
Dispel3
Dispel effects remove normal buffs from the target.

Extinguishing Burning Buildings
 If your alliance fails to defend one of its buildings, the building will start burning. After waiting a certain period of time, your <color=#fff117>alliance leader or officers</color> can extinguish the burning building.
Dismantling
<color=#fff117>Alliance leaders</color> can dismantle an alliance building by tapping on it and selecting the View button to open the building's details page. From there, they can tap the dismantle button in the bottom left.m
